# Excluded files manifest

The evidence trail in this repository is copied byte for byte from the
campaign workspace, with the following exclusions. Every exclusion is
either third party material that is cited rather than redistributed,
a build product that the included sources regenerate, a superseded
internal draft, or bulk output that the included code regenerates.

## Third party material, cited and not redistributed

* sources/Nayak-2026-harmonic-sums-two-intervals.pdf (448,980 bytes)
  and sources/Nayak-2026.txt (65,083 bytes, a text extraction of the
  same document). This is Ritvik Nayak's research note, "A Research
  Note on Harmonic Sums over Two Integer Intervals: Intersecting
  intervals and reductions in the disjoint case", May 2026, written
  with assistance from GPT-5.5 Thinking, announced by the author on
  the erdosproblems.com forum thread for Erdős Problem #288 on
  3 May 2026. It is another author's document and is cited, not
  copied. The campaign's audit of it is AUDIT-A.md.

## Build products, regenerated by the included sources

* code/c0-impl2/prime_kernel.so (20,600 bytes) and
  code/c1-implA/prime_kernel.so (20,728 bytes): shared libraries
  compiled from the included prime_kernel.c. Both census scripts
  rebuild the library automatically with gcc on first run when it is
  absent (see REPRODUCE.md).
* code/c1-implB/engine (59,424 bytes): the compiled binary of the
  included engine.c. Rebuild command:
  gcc -O3 -march=native -o engine engine.c (see REPRODUCE.md).
* Python bytecode caches (__pycache__ directories and .pyc files,
  6 files, 100,644 bytes in total at packaging time): regenerated by
  the interpreter on every run. Bytecode sizes vary between runs; the
  category, not the exact byte total, is the exclusion.

## Bulk output over the size guideline, regenerated by the included code

* code/c1-implA/outputs/solutions_n20000.json (3,291,759 bytes): the
  candidate record of Implementation A at the full cap of 20,000.
  Regenerated by rerunning code/c1-implA/censusA.py with --n 20000
  (see REPRODUCE.md). Its contents are summarised in
  code/c1-implA/RESULTS.md and certified in CENSUS1.md; the
  corresponding record at the 5,000 gate, solutions_n5000.json
  (810,942 bytes), is included.

## Superseded internal draft

* CYCLE1_DESIGN_DRAFT.md (7,373 bytes): a background prepared design
  draft that the controlling design, CYCLE1_DESIGN.md, superseded
  before launch. It records no results and is omitted so that the
  design of record is unambiguous.
